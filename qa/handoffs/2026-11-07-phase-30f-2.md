## 1. What changed

A visual change only (D-122). No word, label, role, route, API or data changed, except one new
control: **Show all N** on the Materials warning.

- **Key figures:** Operations' and Quality's tiles share one look (label, large figure, one or two
  muted lines; "No earlier data vs previous 14 days" now reads on one line). Figures inside cards
  (FM home Rolls today, Operations Materials at risk and Checkpoint before cutting, the Admin
  home's Audit log count, Today's batches) use the same figure size.
- **Page headers:** buttons sit at the right of the title on wide windows (Reports' CSV buttons,
  Inventory's Update stock and Add item). The roll page, the item page and a scan's page use the
  shared header: back link, title with its badge beside it, one description line, actions at the
  right. The item page's On hand sits at the right of the title; Update stock, Edit and Deactivate
  sit together under the header. Add item, Edit, Update stock and Plan days get the same back link.
- **The roll's page:** its details, "How damaged is this roll?" and Shots are cards.
- **Admin home:** Manage users, Lunch break and Open the audit log moved to the top right of their
  cards; Camera API tokens stays at the bottom of the Audit log card, under a line.
- **FM home:** Rolls today shows its three figures in two columns; cards in a row no longer stretch
  to the tallest one; Daily summary's two links sit under a line.
- **Tables at 1280 px:** cells are a little tighter. Reports → Rolls (supplier and date wrap, the
  item name under the roll code is cut with "…" and shows in full on hover), Operations →
  Production (headers on two lines, the note narrower), Materials → Used per material (the "May
  run out…" badge under the days left), Settings → Defect types (When found, Counts toward the roll
  and Description wrap). No column was removed.
- **Materials:** the orange warning names the first five materials, then **Show all N** opens the
  full list in place.
- **Plan days:** each planned day reads "2 rolls · 128 pairs", the interval, then the rolls
  scanned under a thin line; today has a ring; weekends and past days are quiet.
- **API tokens:** Revoke… is a quiet red text button (same name, same dialog).
