## 1. What changed

Run 1 had one app fault (F1, U1): a table wider than its card was a scroll box that a keyboard
couldn't reach (axe `scrollable-region-focusable`, serious) on `/production/plan` and the pages
the smoke spec visits, and the Batches table looked clipped at 375 px. All five e2e failures were
this one issue.

- Every table's scroll box (`src/components/ui/table.tsx`) is now a `ScrollRegion`
  (`src/components/ui/scroll-region.tsx`): full width of its card, scrolling sideways inside it.
  **Only while the table is wider than the box** does the box take keyboard focus (`tabIndex=0`)
  with `role="region"` and a name like "Schedule history, scrolls sideways", so the arrow keys
  scroll it. A table that fits adds no Tab stop.
- Named: Today's check-ins, Production by day, Schedule history, Quota history, Batches. Other
  tables say "Table, scrolls sideways".

No other change. No migration.
