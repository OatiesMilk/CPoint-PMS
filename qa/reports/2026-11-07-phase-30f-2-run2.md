## 4. Failures

### Finding 1 — R3: Rolls table exceeds its 1280 px scroll region

- **Severity:** Medium
- **Role / page:** Floor Manager / Reports → Rolls
- **Steps to reproduce:** 1. Open Reports → Rolls at 1280 × 800. 2. Compare the table scrollWidth with its scroll region clientWidth.
- **Expected:** Equal widths and the full last column visible; status badges wrap to at most two lines.
- **Actual:** Table width is 999 px and the region is 958 px. The Actions column is clipped. Returned to supplier occupies three lines.
- **Evidence:** artifacts/2026-11-07-phase-30f-2-run2/capture-and-measurements.json and fm-reports-rolls-1280-light.png.
- **Suggested fix:** Adjust the Rolls table and status-badge width allocation so Actions stays visible and the long status uses at most two lines at 1280 px.

### Finding 2 — R4: filtered CSV download filename does not match expectation

- **Severity:** Low
- **Role / page:** Floor Manager / Reports → Rolls
- **Steps to reproduce:** 1. Set Status to Returned to supplier at 1280 px. 2. Download CSV.
- **Expected:** A filtered export named rolls.csv.
- **Actual:** The status filter is preserved and all exported statuses are Returned to supplier, but the browser suggests cpoint-rolls-2026-11-07.csv.
- **Evidence:** artifacts/2026-11-07-phase-30f-2-run2/capture-and-measurements.json and rolls-filtered.csv.
- **Suggested fix:** Use the expected rolls.csv download name while continuing to apply active filters.
