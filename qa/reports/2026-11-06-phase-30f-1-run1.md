## 4. Failures

### F1 — S2: Capture marker is more obscured at 390 px

- **Severity:** Medium
- **Role / page:** Shoe Constructor, `/camera/capture`, 390 × 844 phone viewport.
- **Steps to reproduce:** 1. Open Capture at 390 × 844 with the marker visible in the live feed. 2. Compare the baseline and after captures.
- **Expected:** The fixed Capture control leaves the marker visible in the preview to the same extent as the baseline.
- **Actual:** In the baseline a small white edge of the marker protrudes below the fixed Capture bar. In the after screenshot the preview sits higher and the bar covers that marker edge completely. Scrolling can move the preview, but it is hidden in the initial phone view.
- **Evidence:** [Baseline](artifacts/2026-11-06-phase-30f-baseline/sc-capture-390-light.png) · [After](artifacts/2026-11-06-phase-30f-1/sc-capture-390-light.png).
- **Suggested fix:** Preserve enough vertical room between the mobile camera preview and the fixed bottom Capture bar; likely inspect [capture-client.tsx](../../src/app/%28app%29/camera/capture/capture-client.tsx#L889) and the shared header spacing.

### F2 — S9: Card-link arrow moves with reduced motion enabled

- **Severity:** Low
- **Role / page:** Floor Manager, `/floor-manager`, Windows reduced-motion preference enabled.
- **Steps to reproduce:** 1. Turn Windows Accessibility → Visual effects → Animation effects Off. 2. Hover the Open card link.
- **Expected:** The link may fade, but its arrow remains stationary.
- **Actual:** `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is true, yet the arrow's computed translation changes from `none` to `2px` while hovered. Its transition duration is 1 ms. The dialog opened and closed successfully; no distinct dialog movement failure was observed.
- **Evidence:** [S9 measurements](artifacts/2026-11-06-phase-30f-1/s9-reduced-motion.md) · [CardLink](../../src/components/ui/card-link.tsx#L31) · [motion media rule](../../src/app/globals.css#L101).
- **Suggested fix:** Put the arrow translation behind a motion-safe preference and keep reduced-motion styles stationary.
