## 4. Failures

### F1 — L4: a 21-label A4 sheet spans two pages

- **Severity:** Medium
- **Role / page:** Floor Manager, roll-label print view.
- **Steps to reproduce:** Open the in-app roll-label view for 21 in-stock rolls and save as A4 PDF at 100%.
- **Expected:** One A4 sheet with 3 columns × 7 rows (21 labels).
- **Actual:** Page 1 contained 18 labels; page 2 contained the remaining 3. The two-location-label and 14-roll-label PDFs were each one page. jsQR read all 37 label QR images: `LOC:R1-B1`, `LOC:RETURN`; `LR-BSB-039` through `LR-BSB-052`; and `LR-BSA-115` through `LR-BSA-135`. Each payload matched its label.
- **Evidence:** [PDF pagination log](artifacts/2026-11-19-phase-32-run1/l4-pdf.log), [decoded payload list](artifacts/2026-11-19-phase-32-run1/l4-qr-payloads.log), [page 1](artifacts/2026-11-19-phase-32-run1/full-sheet-21-labels-1.png), [page 2](artifacts/2026-11-19-phase-32-run1/full-sheet-21-labels-2.png).
- **Suggested fix:** Review the print page box and row height in `src/app/globals.css`. Seven 42.3 mm rows require 296.1 mm; a 12.9 mm top margin leaves 284.1 mm of A4 height.

### F2 — L10: bulk-move origins are missing from the audit Before column

- **Severity:** Medium
- **Role / page:** System Admin, Audit log.
- **Steps to reproduce:** As Floor Manager, move 051 and 052 from R9-B1 and 050 from R1-B2 to R2-B2 with note “Rack full”. As Admin, filter the audit log to `roll.move` and expand that entry.
- **Expected:** Previous locations appear in Before and R2-B2 appears in After.
- **Actual:** Before displayed “—”. After showed `to = R2-B2`; the roll array included origins R9-B1, R1-B2 and R9-B1. The separate roll histories were correct, but the audit entry had no Before payload.
- **Evidence:** [In-app audit observation](artifacts/2026-11-19-phase-32-run1/l10-audit.log).
- **Suggested fix:** Include the previous roll/location pairs in the bulk-move audit Before payload in `src/server/services/roll.service.ts`.

### F3 — L12: the full E2E suite has nine failures

- **Severity:** High for the regression check; the run is not green.
- **Role / page:** Multiple roles and pages on the working-tree app at port 3001.
- **Steps to reproduce:** Run the five targeted specs in the command above, then run `npm run test:e2e` against the same app.
- **Expected:** The targeted regression specs and full suite pass.
- **Actual:** Targeted run had three failures: `inventory.spec.ts:336` hit `net::ERR_ABORTED` navigating to `/inventory`; `returns.spec.ts:182` found two matches for `getByText('Over the 10% limit')` (the return banner and the detail value); and `rolls.spec.ts:95` timed out after 30 seconds during AxeBuilder `frame.evaluate`.

  The full run had nine failures:

  - `admin.spec.ts:136` (D-093): 30-second timeout; `apiRequestContext.post` reported “Target page, context or browser has been closed” while posting a scan.
  - `admin.spec.ts:216`: supplier-deactivation assertion expected “Inactive” but the row remained “Active”.
  - `capture-no-card.spec.ts:76` (D-121): expected `/camera/capture`; navigation went to `/login`, then `/`.
  - `inventory.spec.ts:267` (D-103/D-104): expected on-hand “1 roll”, received “2 rolls”.
  - `inventory.spec.ts:336` (D-100): expected `/batches` after sign-in; remained at `/login` until timeout.
  - `reports.spec.ts:80` (D-117): expected `/floor-manager` after sign-in; remained at `/login` until timeout.
  - `returns.spec.ts:121` (D-116): timed out navigating to the generated roll detail page.
  - `rolls.spec.ts:28`: timed out waiting for the “Find” field.
  - `rolls.spec.ts:101`: expected “Removed: Water damage in storage”; text was not found.
