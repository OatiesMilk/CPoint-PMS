## 4. Failures

### F1 — S30: Request form fields do not stack at 960px

- **Severity:** Low.
- **Role / page:** Shoe Constructor; `/procurement/new`; 960 × 900 viewport.
- **Steps to reproduce:**
  1. Sign in as constructor@cpoint.local.
  2. Set viewport width to 960px and open /procurement/new.
  3. Look at Item and Quantity in the What you need section.
- **Expected:** Per S30, form fields stack; no sideways page scroll, and list content wraps or scrolls within its own box.
- **Actual:** Item and Quantity remain in adjacent columns. Form and list have no sideways page overflow; only the stacking expectation fails.
- **Evidence:** `qa/reports/artifacts/2026-10-11-phase-11-material-requests/S30-form.png`; `S30-list.png`; `S30.png`. No browser console error accompanied the layout mismatch.
- **Suggested fix:** In `src/app/(app)/procurement/request-form.tsx:250`, the item row switches to multiple columns at the `sm:` breakpoint. Use a breakpoint/layout condition that retains a single column at 960px if that is the desired acceptance behavior; review the custom-item row at line 326 as well. Alternatively, clarify S30 if this compact two-column layout is intended.
