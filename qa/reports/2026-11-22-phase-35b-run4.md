- **Expected:** Repetitions have isolated or coordinated fixtures, so marking Urgent closes the dialog and all eight selected cases can complete.
- **Actual:** Both oldest-first cases passed. One repetition then failed to close Mark Urgent within the unchanged 10,000 ms expectation. Its dialog showed **“B-261115-1 was changed by someone else. The normal single-spec run and post-repeat isolated recheck both passed 4/4; the repeated-run failure remains recorded.
- **Evidence:** `inspection-queue-repeat.log`, `inspection-queue-repeat-results.json`; `e2e-inspection-queue-repeat/inspection-queue-the-Floor-07f3f-to-the-top-and-the-QI-can-t-chromium/error-context.md`; the spec's shared `DAYS`, setup/cleanup and priority changes.
