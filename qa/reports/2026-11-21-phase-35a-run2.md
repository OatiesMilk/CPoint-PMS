## 4. Failures

### F1 — P10: Operations and Quality charts are blank in A4 PDFs

- **Severity:** High. Both reports lose their main charts in the printed output.
- **Role and pages:** Floor Manager, Operations and Quality, November 2026. Operations in both themes.
- **Steps to reproduce:** Open either report at 1280 × 900 with `period=month&year=2026&month=11`. Wait for network idle, the chart SVG and date ticks. Call `page.emulateMedia({ media: 'print' })` and save an A4 PDF with `preferCSSPageSize: true`. Inspect p1.
- **Expected:** The printed chart fits the page and retains the displayed date range, axes and plotted data.
- **Actual:** The chart heading, legend and caption appear, but the plot and date axis are blank. Both SVGs measured 918 × 256 px on screen. In print emulation the wrappers and SVGs measured 0 × 0 px even though the parent containers measured 1,238 px wide and the viewBoxes, ticks and paths were present. Operations dark has the same failure. After E2E finished, a second light capture waited for fonts and two animation frames after switching to print media. Both plots were still blank. Table data prints separately.
- **Evidence:** P1 renders linked above for both light charts and Operations dark. [Settled Operations p1](artifacts/2026-11-21-phase-35a-run2/operations-light-settled-page-01.png) · [Settled Quality p1](artifacts/2026-11-21-phase-35a-run2/quality-light-settled-page-01.png) · [Settled screen and print geometry](artifacts/2026-11-21-phase-35a-run2/settled-chart-observations.json)
- **Suggested fix:** Inspect SVG and chart-wrapper sizing under the print CSS and verify the fix against a populated A4 PDF. The isolated print tests passed with a report header, remarks, signatures and a PDF attachment. They did not assert that a populated chart was visible in the saved PDF.

### F2 — P10: Materials prints the collapsed warning disclosure

- **Severity:** Low. The rest of the table and warning are readable.
- **Role and page:** Floor Manager, `/reports/materials`, with more warnings than fit in the alert preview.
- **Steps to reproduce:** Open Materials without expanding the warning. Emulate print and save the A4 PDF. Inspect p1.
- **Expected:** The stock warning can print, with no screen controls.
- **Actual:** The printed alert contains its disclosure triangle and underlined “Show all 26” control. View, Next and Previous are hidden. The page count line in p3 is intentionally retained.
- **Evidence:** [Materials p1](artifacts/2026-11-21-phase-35a-run2/materials-light-page-01.png) · [Materials p3](artifacts/2026-11-21-phase-35a-run2/materials-light-page-03.png)
- **Suggested fix:** Hide the disclosure control in print and decide how much of the warning list should appear on paper.
